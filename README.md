Notnik is a desktop player for the music already on your computer, and for open catalogs you are allowed to save. The note in the logo is on purpose: this is music, not a notebook.

### Download

- [Windows 64-bit installer, 0.0.5](https://github.com/ZA-MA/notnik-releases/releases/latest/download/Notnik_0.0.5_x64-setup.exe)
- [All releases](https://github.com/ZA-MA/notnik-releases/releases/latest)

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may ask you to confirm. That is expected.

### Listen

Your folders become one library. The same track from two places stays one row, and the extra copies are still on disk. Make playlists, line up a queue, and pick up what you played recently.

Pull the player out into a small window and leave it on top. Or let Notnik sit in the tray and start with Windows.

### Find something new

Jamendo, the Internet Archive, and Wikimedia Commons. When a track can be kept, it downloads into your library. The Internet Archive gives you the whole file, not a short preview.

iTunes Search stays off. It only offers a thirty-second clip, and nothing you can save.

### Make it yours

Dark or light. Russian or English. The window follows its width instead of locking to one size.

When a newer version is published here, Notnik tells you and saves the installer to your Downloads folder. You run it when you want. It does not install itself.

### Stack

A desktop app: Tauri 2, React, and Ant Design.
